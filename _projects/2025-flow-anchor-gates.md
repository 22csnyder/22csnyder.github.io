Have you ever feld that your computer was not being completely forthcoming as to the data it stores? 

Analysis of the CD45xSSC plot is one such instance where most software will freely allow you to misgate populations to catestrophic effect, despite storing phenotypic evidence as to the nature of the misgated events which it could have used to warn you.
